This notice describes cookies and similar technologies used by FEFE Connect and the providers that support the service.

1. Current public preview

The current FEFE public preview does not set FEFE analytics, advertising, preference, or local-storage identifiers. GitHub Pages and ordinary internet infrastructure may process request information such as IP address, requested file, browser details, and timestamp to deliver and protect the site.

2. Necessary technologies planned for member services

When member services activate, Microsoft Entra External ID may use necessary authentication, security, session, and fraud-prevention technologies. Stripe may use necessary payment, checkout, authentication, and fraud-prevention technologies on Stripe-hosted pages. These providers may act independently for some security or legal purposes under their own notices.

3. Analytics and advertising

FEFE does not currently use advertising cookies or cross-context behavioral advertising. FEFE will not activate non-essential analytics, personalization, or advertising technologies without updating this notice and providing a consent or opt-out control where required. A non-essential technology will remain off until the user makes the required choice.

4. Controls

You can configure your browser to block or delete cookies. Blocking technologies required for authentication or checkout may prevent those features from working. When FEFE adds an in-product preference center, this page will link to it and identify each technology’s provider, purpose, type, and duration.

5. Updates and contact

We will update this notice before materially changing FEFE’s use of cookies or similar technologies. Questions can be sent to hello@fefeconnect.com.
