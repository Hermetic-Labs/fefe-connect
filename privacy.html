Privacy principles

Professional connection
without unnecessary exposure.

This placeholder describes the product principles for the preview. Formal terms and a reviewed privacy policy are required before launch.

Collect only what the network needs

FEFE Connect is designed to verify professional membership, present profiles, manage subscriptions, and facilitate introductions. It should not collect clinical records, patient information, case files, or privileged legal material.

Keep profiles member-only

Professional profiles and connection tools are intended for approved members. Applicants remain private until their review is complete.

Use trusted providers carefully

Payment information should be handled by Stripe. Credential and business checks should use authoritative sources. Each provider must be documented in the final privacy notice.

Give members control

Members should be able to update profile details, manage contact preferences, cancel membership, and request account deletion.
