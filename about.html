Founder Lorraine Tillman, LMSW

Meet the founder

A career spent helping people
move forward.

Lorraine Tillman created FEFE Connect around a simple belief: capable professionals should be able to find one another with greater clarity, confidence, and care.

Her background in mental health and her understanding of the legal systems families often encounter gave her a clear view of the gap between the two professions. FEFE is her answer—a thoughtfully reviewed professional network designed to make collaboration easier without making promises it cannot keep.

Experience behind the idea

Built from work
with real people.

Lorraine has more than 15 years of clinical experience in inpatient and outpatient settings. Her work has included young adults, individuals, couples, and families navigating behavioral-health concerns, substance-use challenges, grief, relationship strain, life transitions, and periods of crisis.

She earned a Master of Social Work in Advanced Clinical Practice from Walden University, graduating magna cum laude, after completing a Bachelor of Science in Social Science at Mercer University and an Associate in Applied Science from Hudson Valley College in Albany, New York.

Her practice has emphasized an adaptable, person-centered approach: understand the situation, recognize the strengths already present, and choose a path that fits the person rather than forcing the person to fit a process. That same principle informs how FEFE is being built.

Lorraine’s approach

Clarity first.
Compassion always.

People are better able to move forward when they can draw on their own strengths in an environment of guidance, support, and respect.

The founder’s vision

A smaller, more thoughtful
professional room.

FEFE Connect is designed to help legal and mental-health professionals discover credible peers and begin considered professional conversations. Member applications are reviewed, selected public records are checked, and profiles are kept inside the membership community.

FEFE is not a law firm, healthcare provider, licensing authority, referral service, or guarantee of professional fit or outcomes. Members retain responsibility for their own judgment, diligence, services, and professional obligations.
